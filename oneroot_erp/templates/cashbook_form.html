Quick Rules

Keep The Book Clean

  1. Use one row for each real movement of money.
  2. Use `Reference` for bank alert, teller, MoMo, or receipt number whenever possible.
  3. Use `Notes` to mention the matching source or destination account for deposits, withdrawals, and transfers.

When To Use It

Cashbook Vs Expense

Use `Cashbook / Bankbook` for the movement of money itself. Use `Expenses` when you need the spend description, category, vendor, and receipt trail.
