The tenant must keep the suite and immediate surroundings clean, cooperate with the property's sanitation arrangements, and keep the main gate locked.

9. Repairs, Maintenance, And Access

The landlord is responsible for structural safety and repairs not caused by the tenant's misuse or neglect. The tenant is responsible for promptly reporting faults and for the reasonable cost of damage caused by the tenant, occupants, or guests beyond fair wear and tear. Non-urgent reports may be sent by WhatsApp or text. Urgent leaks, electrical faults, fire, or safety risks must be reported immediately. Except in an emergency, for agreed repairs, or where entry is authorised by law, the landlord will give reasonable advance notice before entering. The tenant will allow access at a reasonable time for inspection or necessary repair.

10. Termination, Renewal, And Possession

This fixed term ends on the stated expiry date unless renewed in writing or otherwise continued under applicable law. Either party may give written notice of a proposed non-renewal or early termination, but no notice in this agreement reduces any longer notice period required by Ghanaian law. The landlord may not lock out, remove the tenant's belongings, disconnect essential services, or recover possession except through the lawful process. Any recovery of possession, dispute about arrears, or claim for breach must follow the applicable Rent Act, Rent Control procedures, and court process.

11. Dispute Resolution And Governing Law

The parties will first try to resolve concerns respectfully in writing. If unresolved, either party may refer the matter to the Rent Control Department, use a lawful alternative dispute-resolution process, or seek the appropriate court remedy. This agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Ghana. Any term inconsistent with mandatory Ghanaian law is read down or replaced only to the extent necessary, without affecting the rest of the agreement.
