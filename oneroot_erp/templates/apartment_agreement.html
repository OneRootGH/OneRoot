The landlord is responsible for structural safety and repairs not caused by the tenant's misuse or neglect. Except in an emergency, for agreed repairs, or where entry is authorised by law, the landlord will give reasonable advance notice before entering. The tenant will allow access at a reasonable time for inspection or necessary repair.
