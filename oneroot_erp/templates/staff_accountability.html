Daily Control Checklist

  • Named cashier and backup for each shift; another person witnesses the count.
  • Record every sale, authorised issue and stock transfer.
  • Receiving officer checks transfers and deliveries.
  • Manager checks stock spot counts, expiry dates, food waste and overdue service payments.
  • Owner reviews exceptions independently. Do not invent missing counts or deduct unexplained differences from salary.

Sales-per-hour targets require reliable shift hours and counter assignment. Targets are not fabricated from incomplete records.
